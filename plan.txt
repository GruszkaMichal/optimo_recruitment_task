Living room renovation plan

My living room needs a new floor, wall painting, and new equipment.

step 1: demolition and floor preparation
- clear all furnitures and electronic in room. 
- protecting other parts of the apartment from dust using plastic sheeting, as well as convering the windows
- preparation of construction materials 
- removal of all floor panels and their disposal
- preparation construction materials for new flooring installation 
- laying new tiles and waiting 2 days
- cleaning the floor and using plastic sheeting to protect them

lead time - 4 days

step 2: wall preparation and painting 

- sendign the walls to remove old paint
- application of paint to the walls using a paint sprayer
- About 15 minutes later, paint the second coat
- removing all protective coverings the next day from walls, floor and windows

lead time - 2 days 

step 3: new furnishing 

- bring in new furniture, including a comfortable sofa, tv, and dresser
- final cleanig room and arrange equipment

lead time - 1 day
